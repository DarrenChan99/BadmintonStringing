Choosing your tension

24 lb — power & comfort28 lb — control & precision

Lower tensions give a bigger trampoline effect and softer feel on mishits. Higher tensions trade power for tighter control and better feedback. Not sure? Tell us your level and swing style when you drop off and we'll suggest a number.
